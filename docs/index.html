Comprehensive Care Management

Everything you need to coordinate professional home care services

Client Location Management

Track multiple client sites with GPS coordinates, interactive OpenStreetMap integration centered on Takaka, NZ. Visual site overview with detailed client information and special needs flags.

Care Action Tracking

Comprehensive care action logging with automated deletion hiding, task completion tracking, site visit recording, and complete audit trails for all care activities.

NZ Compliance Tracking

Built-in compliance management for New Zealand care providers including HDS registration, insurance tracking, privacy audits, and statutory record retention requirements.

Team Coordination

Multi-user support with role-based permissions. Admin and employee access levels, secure authentication, and collaborative workflow management with activity tracking.

Task Scheduling

Advanced task scheduling with calendar views, priority management, automated reminders, and team assignment. Track task completion and monitor care delivery in real-time.

Real-time Sync

Firebase-powered real-time data synchronization across all devices. Offline support with automatic sync when connection is restored. Multi-device collaboration with instant updates.

Special Needs Flags

Track client-specific requirements including wheelchair accessibility, medical equipment on-site, dietary restrictions, mobility assistance, and 12 other special care flags for comprehensive client profiling.

Analytics & Reports

Comprehensive reporting with data visualization, care metrics analysis, compliance status tracking, and exportable reports for regulatory requirements and operational insights.

Mobile Responsive

Fully responsive design optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Access client information, update care notes, and complete tasks from any device, anywhere.
